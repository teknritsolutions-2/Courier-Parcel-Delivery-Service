Behind the estimate

No hidden math.

Understand the weight, distance and service charges behind your estimate.

What is volumetric weight?

Length × width × height in centimeters ÷ 5,000. The divisor is configurable. The calculator compares this result with actual weight and uses the larger value.

How are weights and zones charged?

Billable weight rounds up to the next 0.5 kg. The base includes the first 0.5 kg. Local / Domestic / Express increments are ₹18 / ₹32 / ₹55 per additional 0.5 kg. For straight-line distances over 1,000 km, Domestic adds ₹35 and Express adds ₹55.

Are taxes or carrier surcharges included?

No taxes, official surcharges or paid extras are configured. The estimate shows the base, weight and distance charges.

Can I estimate a same-city parcel?

Yes. Local requires the same pickup and destination city. Domestic and Express also accept same-city routes using their own indicative tariffs.
