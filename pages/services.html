Before your parcel leaves

Give it a good start.

Thoughtful packaging protects the contents and helps make shipment details accurate.

Pack for the journey

Use a rigid box, wrap items individually, fill empty spaces, and tape all seams. Remove old labels and attach a legible recipient label. Measure and weigh the complete packed parcel.

Check before you send

Do not submit hazardous, unlawful, explosive or flammable goods. Batteries, liquids, perishables, valuables and fragile items may require special handling. Obtain the actual carrier’s acceptance policy first.

How is my parcel estimate calculated?

The calculator compares actual and volumetric weight, rounds up to the next 0.5 kg and applies the selected service estimate and distance zone.

Can I send a fragile or restricted item?

Use cushioning and a rigid box for fragile items. Acceptance, exclusions and insurance must be confirmed with the actual carrier before shipping.

What happens after I request a pickup?

Review the details and save your request in this browser. Its reference appears in your dashboard and tracking. No courier is dispatched.
