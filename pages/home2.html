06 / Good to know

A clearer answer.
A smoother start.

Useful details before your first request.

Which details help plan my shipment?

Have your pickup and delivery addresses, contact details, packed weight and outer box dimensions ready. Choose a service to see a useful starting estimate.

How is my parcel estimate calculated?

The calculator compares actual and volumetric weight, rounds up to the next 0.5 kg and applies the selected service estimate and distance zone.

Can I send a fragile or restricted item?

Use cushioning and a rigid box for fragile items. Acceptance, exclusions and insurance must be confirmed with the actual carrier before shipping.

What happens after I request a pickup?

Review the details and save your request in this browser. Its reference appears in your dashboard and tracking. No courier is dispatched.
