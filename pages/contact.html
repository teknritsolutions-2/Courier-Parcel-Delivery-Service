Before you ask

A few useful answers.

Is this a live courier service?

This website is a frontend demonstration of the ARCWAY experience. Sample rates and shipment events are illustrative. A real carrier connection is needed to arrange deliveries.

How is my parcel estimate calculated?

The calculator compares actual and volumetric weight, rounds up to the next 0.5 kg and applies the selected sample service tariff and distance zone.

Can I send a fragile or restricted item?

Use cushioning and a rigid box for fragile items. Acceptance, exclusions and insurance must be confirmed with the actual carrier before shipping.

What happens after I request a pickup?

Review your details and save a demonstration request in this browser. You receive a local reference that appears in the dashboard and tracking page. No driver is dispatched.
